# Code of conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Examples of welcome behavior include constructive feedback, empathy, respect
for differing experience levels, and accepting responsibility for mistakes.
Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, or publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate. Report
conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Strangelight-Merser). Reports
will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
